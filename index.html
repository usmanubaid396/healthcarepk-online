Healthcare Pharmaceuticals — Certified Pharmaceutical Manufacturer in Pakistan

Healthcare Pharmaceuticals is a certified pharmaceutical manufacturing company based in Multan, Pakistan, producing high-quality medicines across multiple therapeutic categories for local and international markets.

Our Certifications & Compliance

  • DRAP Registered — Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan
  • cGMP Compliant — Current Good Manufacturing Practices
  • WHO-GMP Certified — World Health Organization GMP Standards
  • GLP Compliant — Good Laboratory Practices
  • ISO 9001:2015 — Quality Management System
  • ISO 14001:2015 — Environmental Management System
  • ISO 45001:2018 — Occupational Health & Safety
  • ISO 17025 — Testing & Calibration Laboratory Accreditation

Our Products

We manufacture tablets, capsules, syrups, dry suspensions, injectables, creams, ointments, and ophthalmic preparations across therapeutic areas including anti-infectives, cardiovascular, analgesics, anti-diabetics, respiratory care, dermatology, and nutraceuticals.

Export & International Markets

Healthcare Pharmaceuticals holds Certificate of Pharmaceutical Product (CoPP) and Free Sale Certificate (FSC) issued by DRAP, enabling export to markets across Asia, Africa, and the CIS region.
